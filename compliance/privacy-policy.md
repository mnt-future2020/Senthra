## Who this notice is for

- **Staff** — office, warehouse and field personnel with an account.
- **Customer contacts** — people at customer organisations with access to the customer portal.
- **Supplier contacts** — people at supplier organisations whose details we hold for procurement.
- **Job planner contacts** — people named on a customer's job pack as the contact for a site visit.

Supplier and planner contacts usually receive their details from the customer or supplier rather than
directly from us; this notice is how we tell you what we then hold.

## What we hold

**If you are a member of staff:** your name, work email, phone number, home address, date of birth,
gender, employee reference, job title, department, joining date, profile photograph, your handwritten
signature if you upload one, notes recorded by an administrator, and your password in hashed form.
We also record which devices you are signed in on — the IP address and browser or app used — and an
audit trail of actions you take in the system.

**If you are a customer contact:** your name, email, phone number, job title, and your password in
hashed form. We record when you last signed in.

**If you are a supplier or planner contact:** your name, email and phone number, and the site address
your work relates to.

**Photographs and documents.** Damage photographs, site photographs and job-pack documents may include
people or personal details incidentally.

## Who we share it with

We use the following service providers. Full detail, including where each operates, is in
[sub-processors.md](sub-processors.md).

- **Cloudinary** — stores photographs, signature images and documents.
- **Google Firebase Cloud Messaging** — delivers push notifications to the engineer app.
- **postcodes.io** — converts a site postcode to map coordinates. Only the postcode is sent.
- **Our email provider** — delivers the messages we send you.
- **Our database and application hosting providers**.

Purchase orders we issue carry the name, job title and signature of the person who issued them and are
sent to the relevant supplier.

**International transfers: TBC.** Some providers operate outside the UK. The safeguards relied on have
not yet been confirmed and will be stated here once they are.

## Cookies

We use two cookies to keep you signed in, and one to remember your display preferences (theme,
accent colour, density). We use no analytics, advertising or tracking cookies, and no third-party
tracking scripts. Full detail is in [cookie-policy.md](cookie-policy.md).
